Operative Hold

Part of the research sequence — Activation — within Line of Failure

It is not clear
whether what holds
is producing
or only continuing.

—

Meaning may stabilize.
Close.
Repeat.
Becomes readable.

Without opening.

—

Sense does not coincide.
When it appears
does not verify.

—

If meaning closes
no sense.
If it stays open
does not adhere.

—

Meaning remains.
Sense not guaranteed.

—

Here distinction does not hold.
Emerges.
Completes.
Do not separate.

—

What endures
what has sense
may not coincide.

—

It is not clear
where it is produced.

—

Does not distinguish the same way.

—

Exposed
spaceform.
