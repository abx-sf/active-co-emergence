Beyond Readability

Part of the research sequence — Activation — within Line of Failure

Meaning emerges in the interval.
As long as the field holds.

When readability wavers
it does not disappear.
Not enough.

—

Meaning continues.
Does not settle.
Is sought.
Completed.

—

It holds.
Does not produce.

—

The system continues.

—

Here distinction fails.
Interpretation.
Projection.
Do not separate.

—

No verification.

—

Operative.
Exposed
spaceform.
